Jan's Ecological Restoration Blog

Welcome to my blog in ecological restoration!!!

The motivation for this blog page is to record participation in ecological restoration activities. By tracking activities and returning to their sites over time, I hope to learn which interventions are effective. A summary of the impact of activities to-date can be found on the Impact page.

{% comment %}

There is also an attempt to quantify the environmental costs and benefits of ecological interventions in order to evaluate the effectiveness.
